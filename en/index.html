Frequently asked questions

FAQ

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the set of techniques that rank your website at the top of Google's organic results. It delivers the best long-term ROI because it attracts customers who are already searching for what you offer — without paying per click.
SEO is a medium to long-term strategy. First results typically appear within 3 to 6 months, but the benefits compound over time and are lasting. Unlike paid advertising, organic traffic doesn't disappear when you stop investing.
SEO generates organic (free-per-click) traffic by ranking your site naturally. SEM (Google Ads) is paid advertising that delivers immediate results. The best strategy combines both: SEM for quick wins while your SEO matures.
We work with businesses of all sizes. In fact, well-executed SEO and digital marketing are precisely the tools that allow small and mid-sized businesses to compete with the big players.
